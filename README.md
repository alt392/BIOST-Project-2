## About

**Project contains two independent analyses: one focused on comparing different treatments for preventing bone loss among postmenopausal women younger than 60 years old and the other analyzing individuals with septicemia or severe sepsis without mechanical ventilation for more than 96 hours and with a major complication or comorbidity from the Medicare Inpatient Hospitals by Provider and Service dataset, focusing on the average amount Medicare pays to the provider for Medicare's share of the MS-DRGbone density.**
